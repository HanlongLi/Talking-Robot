Artoo Playground

Two robots talking over sound. Type a message, hear the transmitter turn it into audio, push it through a noisy channel, and watch the receiver decode it. Both neural networks run in your browser (2.1 M parameters, no server). Turn your sound on.

Over the air

Put this page on two devices. Start listening on both, then say something from one: the other hears it through its microphone, decodes it, and (if enabled) answers out loud. The microphone is unavailable inside some embedded viewers; the public site at github.io supports it.
